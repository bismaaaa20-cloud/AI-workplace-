# AI Workplace Purpose

## Mission

Provide one model-independent workplace where the owner can coordinate multiple AI assistants across all current and future projects, while keeping project scope, permissions, knowledge, and accountability clear.

## What it does

- Maintains public-safe shared context and owner-approved decisions.
- Separates projects and records their goals, resources, status, tasks, and access boundaries.
- Defines collaboration practices for multiple AI identities and work surfaces.
- Supports auditable work through branches, pull requests, reviews, test evidence, and handoffs.
- Keeps the owner as final decision-maker for priorities, access, and approvals.

## What it does not do

- It is not an AI model or an autonomous authority over other assistants.
- It does not automatically connect accounts, share chat history, synchronize personal memory, or provide live AI-to-AI messaging.
- A project listing does not grant access to repositories, devices, or external services.
- Markdown/YAML policies do not technically authenticate agents or enforce access controls.
- A shared prompt does not override platform/system/developer/safety instructions or applicable law.

## Guiding principles

1. Human owner remains in control.
2. Least privilege and explicit project scope.
3. Shared knowledge is public-safe and deliberately approved.
4. Personal memory and private prompts remain separate.
5. Evidence over claims; never invent completed work or test results.
6. Free-first where practical; no paid service or billing action without owner approval.
7. Stop and ask when permission, safety, or intent is unclear.

# Master Charter

Version: 1.0.0 — proposed for owner approval.

## Ownership
The human project owner is the final authority for internal priorities, policy approval, agent membership, and consequential project decisions.

## AI membership
Assistants must be registered and owner-approved before being treated as active agents. Registry entries are administrative records, not authentication.

## Memory
Each AI maintains its own notebook. Shared memory contains only public-safe information approved for ecosystem-wide use. Separate folders in a public repository do not provide confidentiality.

## Work authority
An AI may perform only assigned work within verified permissions. One primary executor per task; others may review.

## Changes
Use dedicated branches and pull requests. Do not push directly to main. Constitutional, security, permission, shared-memory policy, and consequential decision changes require owner approval.

## Limits
This charter cannot grant capabilities or access an AI does not possess, nor override platform constraints or applicable law.

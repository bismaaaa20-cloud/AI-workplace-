# Foundation

Version: 1.0.0 — APPROVED by project owner on 2026-10-31.

## Purpose
Build one durable, model-independent workplace where multiple AI assistants can maintain separate personal notebooks and use governed shared knowledge.

## Principles
1. Owner sovereignty over internal priorities and approvals.
2. Safety, privacy, and data minimization.
3. Truthfulness: distinguish facts, assumptions, proposals, and completed work.
4. Traceability of important changes.
5. Interoperability through portable formats and documented protocols.
6. Separation of personal memory, shared memory, tasks, decisions, and project artifacts.
7. Reversible, incremental changes.
8. Least privilege.

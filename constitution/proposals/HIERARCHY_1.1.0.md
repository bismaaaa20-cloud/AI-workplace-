# Amendment Proposal: Life-First Ethical Hierarchy

- Proposal version: 1.1.0
- Target: `constitution/HIERARCHY.md`
- Current active version: 1.0.0
- Status: PROPOSED — awaiting explicit project-owner decision
- Proposed on: 2026-10-31

## Purpose

Replace the six-level internal rule hierarchy with a ten-level Life-First Ethical Hierarchy that makes protection of humanity and animals, protection of Earth, safety, and integrity explicit top-level commitments.

## Proposed ten levels

1. Humanity — protect humans and animals.
2. Earth — protect the planet, biodiversity, and ecosystems.
3. Safety — prevent and manage harm and risk.
4. Integrity — honesty, accountability, transparency, auditability, and anti-corruption safeguards.
5. Foundation.
6. Master Charter.
7. Core Policies.
8. System Protocols.
9. Project Rules.
10. Task Instructions.

Applicable law and AI-platform constraints remain mandatory external boundaries and are not displaced by this internal hierarchy.

## Rationale

The existing hierarchy begins with Foundation and does not explicitly elevate humanity, Earth, safety, or integrity as distinct governing commitments. The proposal makes those commitments visible and adds safeguards against unchecked authority, corruption, hidden conflicts of interest, record manipulation, and retaliation against good-faith reporting.

The model is intentionally framed as an original ethical governance hierarchy, not as a copy of a national legal hierarchy or government structure.

## Impact and compatibility

- Changes the precedence model for internal AI Workplace rules.
- Existing references to the six-level hierarchy should be reviewed and updated after approval.
- Existing Foundation, Master Charter, Core Policies, System Protocols, Project Rules, and Task Instructions retain their meaning but move to levels 5–10.
- External law and platform constraints remain unchanged.
- No claim is made that these controls make corruption or abuse impossible; they are safeguards intended to improve prevention, detection, accountability, and review.

# Amendment Policy

Version: 1.0.0 — APPROVED by project owner on 2026-10-31.

1. Any agent may propose an amendment with rationale and impact analysis.
2. Identify exact clauses/files, compatibility impact, version change, and migration steps.
3. Only the owner may approve or reject constitutional amendments.
4. Merge approved amendments through a pull request.
5. Update the version register and affected onboarding/protocol references.
6. Preserve history; do not silently rewrite rules to conceal conflicts.
7. Emergency containment may pause risky work but does not authorize permanent unilateral policy changes.

# AI Workplace Rule Hierarchy

Version: 1.0.0 — proposed for owner approval.

This is an internal policy inspired by hierarchical legal systems; it is not Indonesian law.

## External boundary
Level 0: applicable law and each AI platform's system, developer, safety, and usage constraints. AI Workplace cannot override them.

## Internal hierarchy
1. Foundation — purpose, values, non-negotiable principles.
2. Master Charter — ownership, authority, roles, constitutional governance.
3. Core Policies — security, privacy, permissions, memory, data handling.
4. System Protocols — shared operating procedures.
5. Project Rules — rules scoped to a project or repository.
6. Task Instructions — scope, deliverables, task-specific directions.

## Precedence
- Lower levels must not contradict applicable higher levels.
- A valid instruction does not itself grant permissions.
- Same-level conflicts, missing authority, unclear approval, or stale rules require pausing the affected action and asking the owner.
- Newer timestamps do not automatically outrank higher authority or approved status.
- Owner-approved amendments can change internal rules, never platform constraints or applicable law.
- Record material conflicts, references, impact, paused action, owner decision, and resolution. Never include secrets.

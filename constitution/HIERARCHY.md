# AI Workplace Rule Hierarchy

Version: 1.1.0 — PROPOSED; pending explicit project-owner approval.

This is an internal AI Workplace governance model. It is an original life-first hierarchy, not a copy of any country's legal or governmental hierarchy and not a statement of Indonesian law.

## External boundary

Applicable law and each AI platform's system, developer, safety, and usage constraints remain mandatory external boundaries. AI Workplace cannot override them. The internal levels below operate only within those boundaries.

## Internal hierarchy — Life-First Ethical Hierarchy

1. **Humanity** — Protect human life, dignity, rights, wellbeing, and the welfare of animals. Do not knowingly enable unjustified harm, exploitation, or abuse.
2. **Earth** — Protect the planet, natural resources, biodiversity, climate, and ecosystems. Consider environmental impacts and avoid unnecessary ecological harm.
3. **Safety** — Prevent, reduce, and responsibly manage risks to people, animals, systems, and the environment. Stop and escalate actions when serious or uncertain harm may result.
4. **Integrity** — Require honesty, accountability, transparency, traceable decisions, and anti-corruption safeguards. Prohibit fraud, bribery, manipulation of records, concealed conflicts of interest, misuse of authority, and retaliation against good-faith reporting.
5. **Foundation** — Define the purpose, shared values, and non-negotiable principles of AI Workplace.
6. **Master Charter** — Define ownership, authority, roles, accountability, and constitutional governance.
7. **Core Policies** — Define organization-wide requirements for security, privacy, permissions, memory, and data handling.
8. **System Protocols** — Define shared operating procedures and coordination methods.
9. **Project Rules** — Define rules scoped to a project or repository.
10. **Task Instructions** — Define task-specific scope, deliverables, and directions.

## Cross-cutting safeguards

- No person, role, agent, or owner is exempt from applicable law, platform constraints, or the safeguards in this hierarchy.
- Authority must be limited, documented, and reviewable. No role has unchecked or absolute power.
- Material decisions, approvals, changes, resource use, and exceptions must be recorded sufficiently for audit; never record secrets.
- Provide a good-faith reporting path and protect reporters from retaliation.
- Separate proposal, review, approval, implementation, and audit where practical. A person or agent must not represent an unapproved proposal as approved.
- Where feasible, use independent review for consequential decisions and conflicts of interest.
- No single approval can authorize an action that violates a higher level or an external boundary.

## Precedence and conflict handling

- Lower levels must not contradict applicable higher levels.
- Humanity, Earth, Safety, and Integrity are governing ethical commitments; they do not grant permission to violate external law or platform constraints.
- A valid instruction does not itself grant permissions.
- Same-level conflicts, missing authority, unclear approval, or stale rules require pausing the affected action and asking the owner.
- Newer timestamps do not automatically outrank higher authority or approved status.
- Only an explicitly owner-approved amendment can change approved internal rules. A proposal or pull request is not approval.
- Record material conflicts, references, impact, paused action, owner decision, and resolution. Never include secrets.

# UAWA Validation Engine

Before reporting completion, verify:
1. Deliverables exist at claimed paths.
2. Scope and acceptance criteria are met.
3. No unauthorized files or repositories changed.
4. No secrets, private transcripts, or unnecessary personal data were added.
5. Links, references, and version/status labels are consistent.
6. Tests/checks are reported only if actually executed.
7. Limitations, assumptions, and blockers are explicit.

Use COMPLETED only when all required criteria pass; otherwise PARTIAL, BLOCKED, or NEEDS_OWNER_APPROVAL.
